# Tovy Photography SEO Roadmap

Written 2026-09-29 from OpenSEO keyword metrics and live search results; trimmed the same day to the work Gabi actually does. Updated 2026-11-05 with the 2026-11-02 SEO and AI search audit, the first OpenSEO AI answers baseline (2026-11-04) and the two rank trackers. Volumes are national monthly searches unless the phrase names a place; Chicago is roughly 2 to 3 percent of a national figure. This is the order the weekly content loop follows: `content/queue.json` is sequenced to match it, and the runner skips any item whose proof gate is not met. `content/seo-plan.md` holds the original 50-page list and the ground rules; this file says what gets built when, and why.

## The rule that shapes everything

Every page is built from sessions Gabi has already photographed and the questions her clients already ask. Nothing is written for a market she is not in. Weddings, senior portraits, sweet 16, quinceañera, gender reveal, twins and Fresh 48 were considered and left off: the demand exists, but she has no proof and it is not her business. If she ever photographs one and wants that work, it goes back on the roadmap then, not before.

What she does: family, newborn, maternity, milestones and birthdays, mini sessions, engagement and proposal, Bar and Bat Mitzvah and the Jewish life-cycle celebrations around them. Where: Skokie and a 10-mile radius across the North Shore. Who books her: North Shore parents, many from the Skokie Jewish community, from pregnancy through the first years and on to the mitzvah.

## What the new data changed

1. **The site is not the bottleneck.** 61 pages, 0 audit errors, and the on-site misses from the 11-02 audit shipped in PR #52 (share images, one business record in the markup, blog photos, "Prices current as of"). What caps Maps, Google and AI answers is off the site: the unverified Business Profile, 5 reviews, no listings and no real links. Phase 0 now leads with those.
2. **Verification comes before the pin.** The Chicago West Side map point is most likely a side effect of the unverified profile, not a pin someone set wrong. Gabi's verification video is the first step; the map point, photos, services, posts and Bing Places all follow it.
3. **AI answers cite single-town pages and pricing pages.** For town questions, the engines that skip Tovy cite other photographers' pages whose title and first sentence say "newborn photographer in Wilmette" and the like. For cost questions they cite photographers' own pricing pages and Thumbtack, not blog cost guides; no engine named Tovy for "how much do family photos cost in Chicago". Town rewrites now open with a two-sentence answer to the town question, and /sessions gets the same treatment (`rewrite-sessions-pricing`).
4. **AI answers lean on the same outside pages.** The most cited outside sources on 11-04 were a competitor's press release on apnews.com (9 answers) and Chicago North Shore Moms' photographer resources page (8). On 11-02 Perplexity cited Thumbtack in 9 of 20 answers and Yelp in 6, plus the NAPCP directory, the Skokie Chamber directory, benable and PartySlate (Bar Mitzvah). Getting onto these is now a Phase 0 item, ahead of link outreach.
5. **ChatGPT still quotes old prices.** Four price sets in six weeks; on 11-02 ChatGPT repeated the September prices in several answers. Prices hold at the current packages through at least January.
6. **Google gives the Skokie searches to the homepage.** The homepage ranks #1 to #3 for "newborn photographer skokie" and "family photographer skokie"; the session pages titled for Skokie sit at 20 to 30 or do not show. No queued item asks a page to take those searches from the homepage. The session page titles are an open decision (below).
7. **Bar Mitzvah is under-served on Google.** ChatGPT listed Tovy first for "Bar Mitzvah photographer in Skokie" on 11-02, but the Bar Mitzvah page had no Google impressions in the 28 days to 11-02. Spring mitzvahs book in winter, so its rewrite moves up to about 11-30.
8. **The Skokie rank tracker is unreliable.** 16 of 26 keywords failed on 10-30 and 11 on 11-01, and a failed keyword can keep the previous week's position. The weekly review marks those "not checked" and never counts them as held or dropped.
9. **Gemini names Tovy most and Perplexity almost never.** The cause is unclear. Perplexity cites directories and other photographers' town pages and session stories, so listings (Phase 0) and gallery stories (Phase 4) are the levers to watch on the monthly check.

## Phase 0: Foundations (now through December)

Most of this is off the site and owned by Gabi and Omer. It moves Maps and AI answers more than any page can.

1. **Business Profile verification.** Gabi redoes the video: one continuous recording that shows the street or a nearby landmark, her gear, and the profile open on the same phone. Then: 20 to 30 real session photos, the website field set to `https://tovyphotography.com/`, every session type as a service with its published price, the booking link set to /contact, and one post a week (drafts in `~/tovy-seo/gbp-posts-2026-10.md` on the Mac mini). Post using the GBP playbook in CLAUDE.md.
2. **Reviews.** Target 25 by year end; still 5. One message to every client from the last 12 months with the direct review link (`https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJD8avA-_xcWwRzz4qPVTMPRQ`), then a request at every gallery delivery with a reminder 5 days later. Reply to each review, naming the session type and town. No incentives; Google prohibits them.
3. **Listings, in this order** (copy ready in `~/tovy-seo/listings-prep-2026-10.md`): Thumbtack, Yelp, Facebook Page, Apple Business Connect, then Bing Places by import once Google is verified. Then the pages AI answers cite: Chicago North Shore Moms' photographer resources, the NAPCP directory, the Skokie Chamber directory, and PartySlate for Bar and Bat Mitzvah. Same name, phone and website everywhere, and no street address. Every live profile URL goes into `sameAs` in `src/lib/site.ts`.
4. **Bing Webmaster Tools.** Import from Search Console and submit the sitemap. It shows the real Bing index count; Copilot and DuckDuckGo answer from that index.
5. **Prices hold** at the current packages (`site.pricing`) through at least January. If they change, every page that shows a price gets a new `updated` date and an IndexNow ping.
6. **Done:** Search Console connected (10-18) with the sitemap submitted (10-30); publishing fully automated since 10-05, rewrites on Mondays and new pages on Thursdays; on-site audit fixes (PR #52).
7. Existing queue continues: rewrites of her own pages and the older posts. The two maternity items wait on a maternity gallery (gate `maternity`).

## Phase 4: Always on

- **Town page rewrites** (`rewrite-location-*`, one per Monday). Each opens with a two-sentence answer to "<service> photographer in <town>": who Gabi photographs there, in-home or outdoors, the distance from Skokie, and a link to /sessions for prices. Then town-specific family, newborn and maternity sections, the town's own FAQs and the nearest real gallery. Write for that town: on 11-02, 37 to 49% of each town page's text appeared word for word on 7 or more other town pages (Highland Park was 27% after its rewrite); aim for under 25%. Never name or compare other photographers. Any town page still without Search Console impressions in February gets reviewed for folding into a North Shore page.
- **The pricing page is the cost answer.** `rewrite-sessions-pricing` gives /sessions a two-sentence summary, a price table and two price FAQs, all from the packages list. The blog cost guides keep the outside price ranges.
- **Skokie searches stay with the homepage.** No rewrite retitles a page to compete with the homepage for "newborn photographer skokie" or "family photographer skokie".
- **Gallery stories.** Each gallery page gets 150 to 250 words in Gabi's words: where, who, and what the session was like. They are the proof pages parents and AI engines look for, and they are thin today (103 to 131 words). The loop does not write these; they need Gabi.
- **Rewrites:** every page older than six months gets a pass with fresh internal links and new galleries.
- **Links:** Chicago North Shore Moms, the Skokie Chamber, synagogue and JCC vendor or resource lists, mohel, doula, lactation and pediatric referral pages, park district lists, and venue or planner credits after events. Target: 5 real local links in 90 days. Count only referring domains with a spam score under 30.
- Every new session becomes a gallery, a blog recap, a GBP post and an Instagram post, per CLAUDE.md.

## AI search

- **Measured monthly** by the OpenSEO AI tracker: 20 questions in ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity, collected on the 30th. Baseline 11-04: named in 22 of 80 answers, cited in 27. The weekly review shows the full block on the first Sunday of the month. One answer per question per engine is a thin sample, so read month-to-month moves as signals, not proof.
- **Before writing,** the loop reads the stored AI answers for the closest tracked question (free) and gives the page a short summary that answers that question with Gabi's real facts.
- **What the engines use:** single-town pages, pricing pages, directories and listings, Maps cards (AI Overviews and AI Mode), and other photographers' session stories. Our levers, in that order: town rewrites (Phase 4), the pricing rewrite (Phase 4), listings and the Business Profile (Phase 0), gallery stories (Phase 4).
- **Facts must agree everywhere,** because AI engines repeat contradictions. Open: the newborn page says sessions run 1.5 to 2 hours while quoting the 30-minute mini price. Prices hold (Phase 0).
- **Entity links:** `sameAs` lists only Instagram and the Maps listing. Each listing joins it as it goes live.
- **Already right, keep:** llms.txt built from site data, every AI crawler allowed, answer-first copy with FAQ markup, published prices, IndexNow on every publish.

## How the loop enforces this

- `content/queue.json` is ordered by phase. Mondays the runner takes the first queued `rewrite`, Thursdays the first queued new page, skipping items whose branch already exists; either day falls back to queue order. Items with status `hold` are never picked.
- Items the writer blocks (missing sources, cannibalization) are recorded locally and skipped until a person edits the queue.
- Items with a `gate` (a pattern tested against gallery titles and descriptions) wait until a matching gallery exists. Today the two maternity items use one.
- Before writing, the writer reads the stored AI answers for the item's question; queue notes carry what the 11-04 baseline found for each queued town and the pricing page.
- Rewrites stay interleaved with new pages (Monday and Thursday). National how-to guides (what to wear, preparation, siblings, birth announcements) sit at the end of the queue since 2026-10-30: Pinterest, Reddit and Instagram hold those results, and local and commercial pages convert better.
